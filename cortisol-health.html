Cortisol Health

Cortisol is your body's primary stress hormone. It's produced by the adrenal glands and plays a crucial role in regulating energy, focus, and your body's response to stress.

The Cortisol Curve

Naturally, cortisol follows a daily rhythm: it peaks in the morning to help you wake up and gradually decreases throughout the day so you can wind down at night.

When Cortisol Gets Out of Balance

Chronic stress, poor sleep, and other lifestyle factors can disrupt this natural rhythm. You might experience:

  • Morning fatigue or difficulty waking up
  • Energy crashes in the afternoon
  • Feeling "wired" at bedtime
  • Difficulty concentrating
  • Cravings for sugar and caffeine

Supporting Healthy Cortisol Levels

BC Nutra is designed to support your body's natural cortisol rhythm. Combined with good sleep habits, regular exercise, and stress management, it can help you feel more balanced and energized.
